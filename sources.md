## Evidence rules used

- Fictional facts are marked fictional or illustrative. Sources do not establish Rivermere Council or its scenario.
- Government/regulator sources support the UK governance context; they do not constitute legal advice for the hypothetical use case.
- Vendor pages support capability descriptions only. They do not prove a feature is included in a subscription, that systems integrate, or that a configured workflow is effective.
- No TCS, Accenture, NHS, local council, HMRC, Collibra or OneTrust customer case is represented as the source of the project facts.
- The 16-week plan, roles, risk tiers, metrics and approval gates are proposed design choices for this portfolio case, not external standards or measured benchmarks.
